# Tableau Desktop Foundations Course

Study materials for a Tableau Desktop course, preparing for the **Salesforce Certified Tableau Desktop Foundations** exam (formerly Tableau Desktop Specialist).

## 👉 [Open the flashcards](https://samaardi.github.io/Tableau-Desktop-Foundation-Exam-Prep/)

## 📊 [Tableau Reference Tables](Tableau-Reference-Tables.md)

The key comparison tables for the exam, collected in one place: file types, join types, chart recipes, filters, order of operations, dashboard actions, shortcuts and more.

## What's inside

384 flashcards in 27 decks, grouped by the four exam domains. They cover every objective in the official exam guide, from live connections and relationships to dashboards, stories and aggregation. 67 cards are exam-style questions, including a deck of multiple-choice practice questions. Every card was checked against the Salesforce exam guide and Tableau Desktop help for Tableau 2022.3, the version the exam uses.

## How to study

1. Choose decks in the list on the left (on a phone, tap **Choose decks**). Click a domain heading, or a row in the **Progress by exam domain** chart, to study only that domain. The chart's dashed line marks the 48% pass mark.
2. Read the question and answer it in your head.
3. Tap the card (or press **Space**) to flip it.
4. Be honest: click **I knew it** or **Review again**.
5. Later, turn on **Review again only** to practise the cards you missed.

Keyboard shortcuts: **Space** flip · **← →** move · **1** review again · **2** I knew it

Your progress is saved in your own browser only. Use the same browser and device each time, or your marks won't be there.

## About the exam

The **Salesforce Certified Tableau Desktop Foundations** certification is the entry level of the Tableau certifications, followed by Tableau Data Analyst and more advanced credentials. Tableau certifications are run by Salesforce through Trailhead Academy.

- 40 scored multiple-choice and multiple-select questions, plus up to 5 unscored questions
- 70 minutes
- Passing score: 48% (English)
- Registration fee: 75 USD plus tax; retakes are free
- Proctored online or at a test centre
- No notes, internet or Tableau during the exam, so you need to know where menus and options are
- Based on Tableau 2022.3
- No prerequisites; at least 3 months of hands-on Tableau experience is recommended

Exam details can change. Check the official [exam guide](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=005298988&type=1&language=en_US) before you book.

### What the exam covers

| Domain | Weight | Objectives | Decks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Connecting to and preparing data | 23% | Live connections and extracts; `.tds` files and multiple connections; relationships, joins and unions; renaming fields, aliases, geographic roles, data types, default properties | Live connections and extracts · Cleaning and shaping data · Relationships, joins and unions · Field properties |
| 2. Exploring and analyzing data | 37% | Bar, line, scatter, map, combined axis, dual axis, stacked bar, density map, crosstab and highlight table; groups, sets, hierarchies, filters and date filters; sorting, reference lines, quick table calculations, bins and histograms, calculated fields, parameters, totals | Basic charts · Maps · Combined and dual axis · Groups, sets and hierarchies · Filters and order of operations · Dates and date filters · Sorting and totals · Reference lines and analytics · Quick table calculations · Bins and histograms · Calculated fields · Parameters |
| 3. Sharing insights | 25% | Colour, fonts, shapes, animations, mark size and legends; dashboards, interactive elements and dashboard actions; layouts and device-specific dashboards; stories; sharing as PDF or image, publishing, underlying data, PowerPoint | Formatting · Dashboards and layout · Actions and interactivity · Stories · Sharing and exporting |
| 4. Understanding Tableau concepts | 15% | Dimensions and measures; discrete and continuous fields, date parts vs date values; default aggregation and how it changes when dimensions are added | Dimensions and measures · Discrete and continuous · Aggregation and level of detail |

Two more decks cover the **exam format and strategy** and the **Tableau workspace**, and a **practice exam** deck mixes multiple-choice questions from all four domains.

**Tip:** Domain 2 is the biggest part of the exam, about 15 of the 40 questions, so spend the most time on those decks.
